Plan the cost of Portuguese lessons

Put a price and a schedule together. Calculate your own estimate, then confirm the actual terms with the provider.

Start practising

Compare the complete cost of a plan.

A lesson price is only one part of a practice plan. The number of lessons and the length of the plan change the total. Use a current price a provider has actually given you, keep every amount in the same currency, and check which materials or other costs are included before booking.

Total = lesson price × lessons per week × weeks + one-off materials.
This worksheet uses a fixed weekly schedule. For an irregular schedule, calculate separate blocks or ask the provider for a written total.

A fictional worked example

Suppose you enter a price of 100, two lessons each week, four weeks and 25 for one-off materials.

100 × 2 × 4 + 25 = 825.

That is eight lessons plus the one-off materials. These numbers demonstrate the formula; they are not Barbara Sharon’s prices.

Check the terms behind the number

  • Does “lesson” mean the same length for each option?
  • Are materials included or charged separately?
  • Does the quoted price include applicable fees?
  • What happens if you miss or reschedule a session?

Enter any one-off materials cost only once. Ask the provider about other charges rather than assuming this calculator covers them.

Enter your own figures.

Choose one currency for every amount. Currency selection only changes labels and formatting: it does not convert money. Use a dot or comma for up to two decimal places, without thousands separators.
