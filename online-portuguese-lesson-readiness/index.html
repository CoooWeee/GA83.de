Get ready for your first online Portuguese lesson

A small preparation sheet for joining the lesson, explaining your goal and knowing what to ask.

Start practising

Make room for the conversation.

For a first online Portuguese lesson, useful preparation is practical: know how you will join, make the audio usable, and bring one goal you can explain. You do not need to master the lesson content in advance.

Work through the checks below. An unchecked item is a question to resolve with the teacher, not a score for your ability to learn. Use the notes to keep the agreed arrangements in one place.

A useful first-lesson brief

“I know a few greetings. I would like to introduce myself to my partner’s family. Could we practise a short introduction, then discuss what I should review before the next lesson?”

This example gives the teacher a level, a situation and a feedback question. Adapt it to what you actually want to do.

Your first-lesson preparation.

Tick what is ready and note anything to confirm. Nothing is sent to a teacher by this page. Print the checklist and your notes when you are ready.

Audio: Make it easy to hear and be heard.

Before the lesson, use the meeting app’s own settings or test call to check your selected microphone and speaker. A headset may help if your room is noisy. This page does not test devices or request microphone access.

Connection: Know how you will join.

Open the meeting app ahead of time and find the lesson link the teacher has provided. If a connection fails, use the contact method already agreed with the teacher to discuss what to do next. Do not assume an alternative platform is available.

Materials: Keep a small working space ready.

Have a notebook or document for phrases and questions. If the teacher has shared materials, check that you can open them. Avoid spending the first session moving between many unrelated apps.

Scheduling: Confirm the date as well as the time.

Record the lesson date and the teacher-confirmed time in both locations. Time-zone abbreviations can be ambiguous and daylight-saving changes can alter the difference between places. Confirm the actual lesson date with the teacher; this page does not convert time zones.

Learning goals: Bring one situation you want to handle.

Choose a task you can describe, such as introducing yourself to a family member or asking for a drink. Say what you already know and what you would like feedback on. The teacher can help turn that brief into a starting plan.

Keep the arrangements together.

Record only the details you need for this worksheet. Avoid adding passwords or private meeting access codes.

If something is still uncertain.

I cannot hear clearly.
Check the selected output and volume in your own meeting app. Tell the teacher what is happening rather than pretending to understand.
I am unsure about the time.
Confirm the full date, time and location-based time zone with the teacher before the lesson.
I do not know what to prepare.
Ask whether the first session requires materials. A brief goal and an honest description of your current level are useful starting information.
I feel nervous about speaking.
Bring a small introduction and a clarification phrase such as “Pode repetir mais devagar?” — “Can you repeat that more slowly?” Discuss how you prefer to receive correction.

After the session, add one phrase to review and one question for next time. Keep the preparation small enough that you can use it again.
